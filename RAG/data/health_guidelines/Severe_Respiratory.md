# Severe AQI (401–500) — Respiratory

## Overview

When the Air Quality Index (AQI) enters the "Severe" category (401–500), the air pollution has reached catastrophic levels, constituting a public health emergency. According to the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) of India, this level directly affects healthy people and seriously impacts those with existing diseases. The World Health Organization (WHO) warns that at these extreme hazardous concentrations of fine particulate matter (PM2.5) and toxic gases, the environmental conditions are incompatible with normal biological function. For the respiratory population, this is an absolute crisis state. For individuals with preexisting respiratory conditions like asthma, COPD, or chronic bronchitis, a Severe AQI is a catastrophic environment. The CPCB clearly states this seriously impacts those with existing diseases, functioning as a direct, unavoidable trigger for severe, life-threatening exacerbations. At this maximum severity tier, the air is visibly thick with smog, and all normal outdoor daily routines must be halted. Strict indoor confinement, heavy reliance on specialized air filtration, and constant medical vigilance are strictly required to prevent acute, life-threatening physiological events.

## Health Effects

In a Severe AQI environment (401–500), the sheer density of airborne particulate matter completely overwhelms the human body's respiratory and cardiovascular defense mechanisms. Microscopic PM2.5 particles are inhaled in massive, toxic quantities, flooding deep into the alveolar regions of the lungs and rapidly crossing into the bloodstream. The massive influx of particulate matter acts as a lethal irritant to already compromised airways. This exposure rapidly leads to intense bronchospasms, dangerous airway constriction, and heavy mucus production, frequently resulting in total respiratory failure. This triggers extreme systemic oxidative stress, severe airway inflammation, and a rapid decrease in blood oxygen levels. The body enters a state of physiological panic, attempting to fight off the environmental toxins. Even brief exposure can lead to acute respiratory distress, severe coughing fits, sudden spikes in blood pressure, and a drastically compromised immune system, setting the stage for critical medical emergencies and long-term organ damage.

## Outdoor Activities

### Can I go for a morning walk?

Absolutely not. Morning walks are incredibly hazardous and strictly prohibited under Severe AQI conditions. During the early morning hours, temperature inversions create an impenetrable lid over the area, trapping the absolute maximum concentration of toxic smog directly at ground level. Respiratory patients must completely abandon all outdoor walks. Even slow walking in this dense, toxic smog forces irritating particulates into sensitive lungs, instantly triggering violent, uncontrollable coughing and wheezing. Stepping outside for a walk during these peak pollution hours guarantees the inhalation of a massive, concentrated dose of toxic particulate matter, entirely destroying any potential cardiovascular benefits and instead acting as a direct catalyst for immediate respiratory illness.

### Can I go jogging or running?

Jogging and running outdoors are lethally dangerous in a 401–500 AQI environment and must be universally suspended. Sustained aerobic exertion forces the body to consume vast amounts of air through the mouth, entirely bypassing the nose's natural filtration. Running is strictly forbidden for respiratory patients in a Severe AQI. The deep aerobic inhalations will guarantee a severe, acute asthma attack or COPD exacerbation requiring immediate hospitalization. This violent inhalation pulls thick, toxic smog directly into the deepest, most sensitive lung tissues. The combination of intense physical strain and extreme environmental toxicity exponentially increases the likelihood of exercise-induced asthma attacks, acute lung injury, and catastrophic cardiovascular failure. All running must be strictly relocated to a highly purified indoor facility.

### Can I cycle?

Cycling is entirely unsafe and must not be attempted. The physical exertion of cycling combined with sharing road space with motor vehicles places the cyclist in the most dangerous micro-environment possible. Cycling is entirely unsafe and must be avoided. The physical strain combined with catastrophic ambient pollution will rapidly overwhelm the patient's severely compromised respiratory reserves. The direct, prolonged inhalation of concentrated vehicular exhaust layered on top of the already Severe regional background pollution creates a deadly exposure scenario. Individuals must abandon cycling completely and rely solely on enclosed, filtered transportation to protect their immediate health.

### Can I commute?

Commuting should be avoided entirely if possible, with individuals strongly urged to work or study from home during a Severe AQI event. Commuting must be handled as an emergency procedure. Respiratory patients must use closed vehicles with the air conditioning on internal recirculation and should absolutely avoid all open-air transport to prevent direct toxic inhalation. If travel is an absolute, unavoidable necessity, open-air transit—such as walking, cycling, motorcycles, or open-sided auto-rickshaws—is strictly forbidden. Commuters must travel in fully enclosed private vehicles or buses, with all windows hermetically sealed and the air conditioning firmly locked on internal recirculation to block the toxic outdoor air from entering the cabin.

### Can I exercise outdoors?

All forms of outdoor exercise, sports, physical education, and physical labor must be immediately and unequivocally canceled. The metabolic demands of exercise force the body to act like a vacuum, pulling in catastrophic amounts of polluted air. All forms of outdoor exercise must be canceled. Patients should restrict themselves to total indoor rest and keep all prescribed rescue medications, nebulizers, and oxygen supplies immediately accessible at all times. Engaging in physical activity outside in these conditions is an act of severe self-harm. It actively destroys lung tissue and stresses the heart to the point of failure. All physical movement should be minimized and restricted entirely to safe, deeply purified indoor environments.

### Can I spend several hours outside?

No one should spend any prolonged time outside under any circumstances. The CPCB explicitly states that this category affects even healthy people; prolonged exposure is a guaranteed path to acute respiratory illness. Time spent outdoors must be strictly limited to the absolute minimum necessary for life-saving transit. Any exposure will definitively lead to a severe, acute worsening of their chronic lung condition. Spending several hours outside—whether for work, recreation, or socializing—will result in a massive, dangerous accumulation of toxins in the blood and lungs. Total indoor confinement is the only medically sound recommendation until the severe smog clears.

## Should I Wear a Mask?

At a Severe AQI level, wearing a high-grade pollution mask is absolutely mandatory for any essential, brief outdoor transit. An N95, KN95, or FFP2 respirator is the bare minimum requirement to physically block the dense cloud of PM2.5 particles. An airtight N95 or KN95 mask is absolutely mandatory for respiratory patients anytime they leave the house. Without this high-level filtration, the unfiltered air will act as a direct and immediate trigger for respiratory collapse. Standard cloth masks, surgical masks, and scarves are entirely useless and provide a false sense of security. The respirator must be tightly fitted, forming a flawless, airtight seal around the nose and mouth. If air leaks in around the edges of the mask, the user is directly breathing the toxic smog.

## Indoor Recommendations

Creating an indoor sanctuary is a matter of immediate health security. All exterior windows, doors, and vents must be aggressively sealed and kept shut at all times. Homes must be completely sealed from the outside air. Running a HEPA air purifier continuously at maximum speed in the patient's bedroom is non-negotiable to provide a safe, life-sustaining environment. Households must continuously operate High-Efficiency Particulate Air (HEPA) purifiers at maximum capacity in all occupied rooms to scrub the infiltrating PM2.5 from the indoor air. Absolutely all indoor pollution sources must be banned—this includes zero burning of incense (agarbattis), candles, mosquito coils, or wood, and avoiding any frying or heavy cooking that generates indoor smoke, as the house cannot be ventilated.

## Symptoms to Watch

Under Severe conditions, even with strict precautions, severe symptoms can rapidly develop. Patients must be highly alert for a sudden increase in wheezing, a painful, crushing sensation in the chest, uncontrollable coughing, or a sudden, dramatic drop in their peak flow meter readings. Individuals must constantly monitor themselves for acute signs of pollution poisoning: a deep, uncontrollable hacking cough; a heavy, crushing sensation in the chest; severe burning in the eyes and throat; extreme, uncharacteristic lethargy; and noticeable shortness of breath while resting. These are critical biological alarms indicating that the body is failing to cope with the environmental toxic load.

## When Should I Seek Medical Help?

Severe AQI events frequently trigger acute medical emergencies. Immediate emergency medical care is critical if a respiratory patient uses their rescue inhaler or nebulizer and fails to find relief, or if they experience severe breathlessness, confusion, and cyanosis while at rest. Immediate, emergency medical intervention is required if any individual experiences sudden, severe shortness of breath, loud wheezing, sharp or radiating chest pain, blue tinting of the lips or nail beds (cyanosis), or sudden confusion and fainting. These are classic signs of acute respiratory or cardiac failure, requiring urgent oxygen therapy and professional clinical stabilization. Do not delay seeking emergency care if these signs appear.
